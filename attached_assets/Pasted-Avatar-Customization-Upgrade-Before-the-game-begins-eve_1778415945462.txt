Avatar Customization Upgrade
Before the game begins, every player goes through a full avatar customization screen. The screen has a warm vibrant background in deep gold or rich navy to match the investment simulator's theme, and displays the player's 3D-style avatar front and center inside a clean card panel or phone frame mockup. Along the right side of the avatar preview is a vertical icon toolbar with category selectors for face, expression, hat, top or shirt, and bottoms — each rendered as a clean rounded icon button with the active category highlighted in a dark filled state. Below the avatar is a horizontal scrollable tray showing item options for the currently selected category, displayed as small preview thumbnails in rounded cards with a checkmark indicator on the selected item. Players can scroll through and tap to swap items onto their avatar in real time with a smooth swap animation so the change feels instant and satisfying. Include at least these customization categories: skin tone, hair style, facial expression or accessories, hat or headwear, shirt or jersey top with the ability to display a short custom label or text on it, and pants or shorts. Once the player is happy with their look, a bold Ready or Start Investing button at the bottom confirms and locks in their avatar before sending them into the game lobby. The customization data for each player should be saved into the multiplayer session state so that every player's unique avatar is visible to all other players throughout the game and on the final podium screen.

Final Standings Podium Upgrade
Add a beautiful animated Final Standings Podium screen that appears automatically when all investment rounds are complete. The layout shows the top 3 players with 1st place raised highest in the center, 2nd place on the left slightly lower, and 3rd place on the right lowest. Each podium block is a bold glowing card displaying the player's custom avatar, their username, and their final portfolio value formatted as currency or in scientific notation for extremely large numbers. A medal badge sits in the top corner of each card — gold for 1st, silver for 2nd, bronze for 3rd — styled as rounded number tags. The title Final Standings appears at the top in large bold text with a subtle glow effect, and gold coin or stock ticker confetti rains down across the screen when it loads. The podium blocks animate in from the bottom using a staggered spring entrance where 3rd rises first, then 2nd, then 1st, and the 1st place card continuously pulses with a golden glow to celebrate the winner. A Play Again button resets everything back to avatar customization and the lobby, and a View Leaderboard button expands a smooth scrollable list of every other player ranked below 3rd with their avatar, username, and final value.

Live Stock Market Feed Upgrade
Replace any static or fake stock data with a live animated stock ticker system that feels like a real financial terminal. At the top of the game screen add a horizontal scrolling ticker tape showing all available stocks with their current price, percentage change, and a color indicator — green with an up arrow for gains and red with a down arrow for losses. Each round the stock prices should shift dynamically based on randomized market events that are announced to all players simultaneously, such as a tech crash, oil boom, or interest rate hike, shown as a bold news banner that slides in from the top of the screen. Stocks should have realistic volatility — some are stable blue chip stocks that move slowly, some are volatile growth stocks that can spike or crash dramatically in a single round, and some are crypto-style assets that are completely unpredictable. Players should be able to click any stock to see a mini price history chart showing its movement across the last few rounds rendered as a smooth line graph with gradient fill.

Portfolio Dashboard Upgrade
Give every player a rich personal portfolio dashboard that they can open at any time during the game. The dashboard shows a breakdown of all their current holdings with the stock name, number of shares owned, average buy price, current price, total value of that position, and the profit or loss percentage highlighted in green or red. At the top of the dashboard show the player's total portfolio value as a large bold number that animates up or down in real time as prices change. Include a pie chart or donut chart that visualizes how the portfolio is distributed across different stocks and sectors. Add a net worth history line graph that plots the player's total value at the end of each round so they can see their growth trajectory over the entire game. The dashboard should slide in from the side as a smooth panel overlay without interrupting the main game view.

In-Game Chat and Reactions Upgrade
Add a live in-game chat panel on the side of the screen where all players in the multiplayer session can send short messages to each other during rounds. Include a set of quick reaction buttons so players can fire off emoji-style reactions instantly — a rocket for when stocks are pumping, a skull for a bad investment, a money bag for big gains, and a chart going up for market hype. When a player sends a reaction it floats up from their avatar on the main screen in a large animated emoji burst visible to everyone, creating a shared social moment. Limit text messages to 80 characters to keep the chat fast and fun. Add a notification ping sound and a brief highlight flash on the chat panel when a new message arrives so players never miss the conversation.

Market Event Cards Upgrade
Each round introduce a dramatic market event card that pops up in the center of the screen before trading begins, styled like a breaking news alert or a Wall Street memo. The card should have a bold headline such as Federal Reserve Raises Interest Rates, EV Company Announces Record Sales, or Inflation Hits 10 Year High, followed by one or two sentences explaining which sectors are affected and whether the impact is positive or negative. The affected stocks should visually shake or flash on the stock list to signal incoming price movement. Some events should be global affecting all stocks slightly, some should be sector-specific hitting only tech or energy or consumer goods, and rare events should be black swan shocks that cause extreme unexpected swings across the entire market. Players have a short countdown timer after seeing the card to make their trades before the round locks and prices update.

Achievements and Badges Upgrade
Add a hidden achievement system that rewards players for specific in-game milestones and surprises them with a pop-up badge notification when they unlock one. Achievements should include things like First Million for reaching a portfolio value of one million for the first time, Diamond Hands for holding the same stock for three or more rounds without selling, Buy the Dip for purchasing a stock immediately after it drops more than 20 percent, Diversified for owning five or more different stocks at the same time, and Comeback King for going from last place to first place in a single round. Each achievement pops up as a sleek animated toast notification in the corner of the screen showing the badge icon, the achievement name, and a short fun description. At the end of the game the Final Standings screen also shows each player's earned badges beneath their podium card so everyone can see the highlights of each player's journey.

Sound Design and Music Upgrade
Add a full ambient sound layer to the game that makes every action feel alive and reactive. Play a subtle looping background track that sounds like a busy trading floor or a futuristic financial hub, with low energy during the planning phase and building intensity as the round timer counts down. Add satisfying click sounds for buying and selling stocks, a cash register or coin clink for completing a trade, a dramatic chord sting when a major market event card appears, a rising fanfare when a player hits a new net worth milestone, and a confetti burst sound when the podium screen loads. Include a small sound settings toggle in the corner so players can mute everything with one click. All sounds should be short, non-intrusive, and feel cohesive with the futuristic investment theme of the game.
