💰 2025 → 2026 Economic Situation (Game Logic Style)
🟢 2025: Strong but overheated economy

Core vibe: Growth + hidden instability

Key drivers:
High consumer spending
AI-driven investment boom
Strong job markets in major economies
Led by:
Federal Reserve keeping rates relatively high
European Central Bank still fighting inflation
Problems forming:
Inflation not fully under control
Interest rates still high
Asset prices (stocks, tech) becoming overvalued

👉 Result:

📈 Growth continues
⚠️ Economy becomes fragile underneath
🟡 Late 2025: Slowdown begins

Core vibe: Cracks start showing

What changes:
Consumers start spending less
Borrowing becomes expensive
Companies slow hiring
Market signals:
📉 Stock volatility increases
🏠 Housing markets cool
💼 Layoffs begin in some sectors

👉 Result:

🐢 Growth slows
📊 Confidence weakens
🔴 Early 2026: Shock phase

Core vibe: Inflation + external shocks hit

Key trigger:
Energy price spike (oil shock)
Supply chain disruptions return
Institutions reacting:
International Monetary Fund warns about slowing growth
World Bank lowers global forecasts
What happens:
Inflation rises again
Central banks pause or reverse rate cuts
Businesses face higher costs

👉 Result:

⚠️ Risk of stagflation (low growth + high inflation)
🟠 Mid 2026: Stagflation pressure

Core vibe: Worst of both worlds

Conditions:
📉 Slow or near-zero growth
📈 Persistent inflation
💰 High interest rates remain
Effects:
Consumers struggle
Businesses cut costs
Investment slows down

👉 Result:

😬 Economic discomfort phase
Markets become unstable
🔵 Late 2026: Stabilization attempt

Core vibe: Trying to recover

What improves:
Inflation starts easing slowly
Supply chains stabilize
Governments introduce support measures
Central bank actions:
Federal Reserve considers rate cuts
European Central Bank shifts toward growth support

👉 Result:

📊 Slow recovery begins
🧊 Economy stabilizes but remains weak
